Analysis Information

How does I Model analyze your face?

  • Face Landmarking (MediaPipe): Detects facial landmarks to map out specific skin areas (cheek and forehead patches). This guarantees hair, eyes, and background are excluded for skin measurement.
  • Age Prediction (DeepFace): Runs deep learning neural models to estimate chronological skin age.
  • Glow Index Calculation: Evaluates brightness (Luminance V/Y value), smoothness (high-frequency noise via Laplacian filter), and color uniformity to compute a final percentage score.
  • Skincare Defense Routine: Provides customized routines (Eat, Drink, Apply) to protect against dust, dirt, pollution, and pimples.
